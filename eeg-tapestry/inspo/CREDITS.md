All 11 images in this folder are by **Alexandra Szántai** (Szántai Alexandra), from her 2015 master's project
**[Brainwaveit – Brainwave visualisation (Master degree)](https://www.behance.net/gallery/27100775/Brainwaveit-Brainwave-visualisation-%28Master-degree%29)**
